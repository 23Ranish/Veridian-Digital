Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with us.

How long does a website typically take?

On average, a standard business website takes 4-6 weeks from initial consultation to final launch. More complex projects with custom features may take 8-12 weeks. We establish a clear timeline during our strategy phase.

Do you manage my website after it's launched?

We don't currently offer ongoing website management, but we do personally guide you through the launch process and show you how to update and manage your website yourself. You'll receive step-by-step support and won't be left figuring things out alone.

Will my website look great on mobile devices?

100%. We employ a mobile-first design approach. Over half of all web traffic comes from mobile devices, so we ensure your site provides a flawless, responsive experience across smartphones, tablets, and desktops.

What information do I need to get started?

To begin, we'll need a basic understanding of your business goals, target audience, and any existing branding materials (logo, colors). Don't worry if you don't have everything figured out — our discovery process is designed to help define these elements.
